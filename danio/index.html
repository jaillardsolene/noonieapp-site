Danio

Track your aquarium's water, feedings, and maintenance — nothing else.

Danio logs what a freshwater tank actually needs day to day: water parameters (pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, temperature, GH, KH, TDS, plus one custom parameter), water changes, feedings, and livestock — species typed in by hand, no closed database to hit a wall on. Maintenance tasks repeat on the schedule you set, with reminders when something's due.

One tank, unlimited entries, import and export: free. Unlimited tanks and the full parameter charts: unlocked with a monthly, yearly, or one-time purchase — family sharing included on all three.

Everything you enter stays on your device. No account, no server.
