Danio Support

Restore a purchase (new phone, reinstalled the app)

Settings → Restore Purchases. Works for lifetime and subscriptions.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Danio. iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Danio → Cancel.

Refunds

Handled by Apple, not by Danio. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Move to a new device

Your data restores automatically with your iCloud backup, or use Export/Import inside the app.
